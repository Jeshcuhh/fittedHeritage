A thrifting companion

Good clothes deserve a second seam

Tell Second Seam your style, your measurements and what’s already in your closet. It finds thrifted pieces ranked by the things you say matter, plans which goals to buy first on your budget, and points you to a tailor you can trust when a $15 hem is all that stands between you and a perfect fit.

Start Skip setup, just browse

How it works

  1. Say what you like

    Pick looks, garment types and colors. Name the piece you’re hunting for.

  2. Measure once

    Body or a favorite garment. It stays on this device.

  3. Show your closet

    So you stop buying the fourth black tee.

  4. Set a budget

    Every find is checked against it, tailoring included.

  5. Weigh what matters

    Fiber, fit, style, cost. You set the balance.

  6. Plan your goals

    See the chance of filling each one by its deadline.

  • Underwear is always newThe one thing we never send you to a thrift store for.
